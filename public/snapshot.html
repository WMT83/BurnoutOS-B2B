What you get

A pattern map, not a diagnosis.

Most burnout content treats it as a single feeling. The Snapshot treats it as a pattern across four specific dimensions, and shows you which ones are loudest in your life right now.

01 · Energy

Where your tank actually empties

The exhaustion side of burnout — physical, cognitive, and emotional. Where you spend it, where you can't recover it, and what's specifically depleting in your week.

02 · Demands

What's actually being asked of you

Workload, role clarity, emotional load, decision density. Mapped against the JD-R model used in occupational psychology — not against a vague "are you stressed" framing.

03 · Support

What's holding you up — or isn't

Resources, autonomy, relationships, recognition. The protective side of the equation. Burnout doesn't only come from too much demand — it often comes from too little support.

04 · Meaning

Why the work still matters — or doesn't

Purpose, alignment, the gap between what you say is important and what you actually do. The dimension most commonly missed by quick "burnout tests" — and often the most important.

Your 7 days

One short module a day. Then a real report.

Each day takes about 10 minutes — a short read or video, a brief check-in, and the AI coaching companion if you want to think out loud. By Day 7, the data tells a real story.

  1. Day 1

    Baseline + Energy

    A short Burnout Baseline Assessment so we have a real reference point, then the first dimension — where your energy actually goes during a week, and where it doesn't come back.

  2. Day 2

    Demands

    What's actually being asked of you — quantitatively, emotionally, cognitively. Most people overestimate one and underestimate two others; the check-in surfaces that.

  3. Day 3

    Support

    The protective side — autonomy, social support, recognition, resources. What's working and what's been quietly missing.

  4. Day 4

    Meaning

    Whether the work still aligns with what you actually care about. The dimension that quietly drives the other three when it's out of alignment.

  5. Day 5

    Pattern feedback

    Halfway through. What's emerging across the four dimensions? A short reflection from you that feeds into the Day 7 report.

  6. Day 6

    Integration

    One small practice tested in your own life. Not a "habit stack" — one specific, testable thing that the data suggests would matter.

  7. Day 7

    Your Snapshot Report

    A personalised report mapping where your burnout lives across the four dimensions, what the data suggests, and three specific next steps. Yours to keep.
