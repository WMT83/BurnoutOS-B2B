The problem

Burnout is expensive. The bill is invisible.

  • Burnout-related absenteeism, presenteeism, and turnover cost Australian employers an estimated AUD 6,500 per affected employee per year.
  • For an organisation of 200 people with a 23% burnout prevalence rate — the Australian average — that's over AUD 1.3M annually.
  • That number doesn't appear on the P&L. It appears as missed deadlines, underperforming teams, medical leave, and unexpected departures.
  • Without data, it's invisible. Without a dollar figure, it's impossible to justify investment. And without a plan, nothing changes.

Our commitment

If we don't find it, you don't pay.

Every tier carries a scaled ROI guarantee. If the diagnostic does not identify estimated productivity and retention costs above the threshold for your tier — AUD 50,000 (Essentials), AUD 100,000 (Sprint), or AUD 200,000 (Enterprise) — the diagnostic is complimentary.

We offer this because for any Australian organisation above 50 people, the number is almost always significantly above threshold. The guarantee exists to remove the risk from your decision — not because we expect to invoke it.

Condition: minimum 60% workforce participation in the assessment across all tiers. Participation protocols, employee communication templates, and manager briefing notes are provided as part of every engagement.
