Who Phase 1 is for

You're still delivering. But the tank is running low.

Performance Reset is built for capable professionals who are still functioning but feeling the cost. You are not in crisis. You are running on reserves that used to refill faster than they do now. This is the phase that stops the slide and rebuilds the baseline before it becomes something harder to recover from.

Senior leaders

Directors, partners, and executives carrying high responsibility with limited control over the workload. You want fast traction and a rhythm you can actually keep.

Clinicians and allied health

Psychologists, doctors, and health professionals carrying others' load while running on empty. The same tools you use with patients, applied to yourself.

Still functioning, running low

You are coping, possibly better than most. But recovery takes longer than it did, and slowing down has started to feel risky.

Wanting a repeatable rhythm

You have tried the holiday and the long weekend. They helped for a week. You want something structural that holds on a low-capacity Monday.

What is included

Eight weeks. Live coaching. Practical tools.

Each week combines a 60-minute live group coaching session with Werner, a guided exercise, and a weekly self-tracking dashboard. The work is designed to be applied on a busy week, not added to it.

Weekly live group coaching

Eight 60-minute sessions with Werner and a cohort of no more than 12. Topic, guided exercise, and implementation troubleshooting each week.

Recovery protocol toolkit

Sleep anchors, downshift routines, and micro-recovery practices that rebuild your baseline energy without requiring a sabbatical.

Cognitive load skills

CBT and ACT-informed tools for rumination, perfectionism, and the mental overload that keeps you switched on when you should be off.

Boundary and priority scripts

Capacity-signalling language, trade-off phrasing, and meeting-decline scripts. The words to use when "no" feels impossible.

Shutdown ritual and deep work protection

An end-of-day close-down, a next-day top three, and protected blocks that defend the work that actually matters.

Weekly self-tracking dashboard

Track sleep consistency, recovery, workload, and boundary adherence. A real picture of your capacity, week on week.

What you leave with

Three tangible outputs by Week 8.

Overload Map

Your key drivers — demands, control, support, and values friction — mapped so you can see what is actually depleting you.

Personal Recovery Protocol

Your non-negotiables and early warning signs, written down as a protocol you can return to when capacity drops.

Boundary Starter Pack

Calendar rules and ready-to-use scripts for the conversations that protect your time and attention.

How it works

A structured eight weeks, not a content dump.

The programme

Eight weeks, week by week.

One 60-minute live session per week with Werner, a guided exercise, and a practical action to apply before the next session. Designed to work on a busy week, not added to it.

Phase A — Baseline and orientation
01 Wk 1
Burnout Baseline and Overload Map

Review your Burnout Baseline results as a group. Map the key drivers specific to you: demands, control, support, and values friction. You leave knowing exactly where your depletion is coming from, not guessing.

✓ Session 1 — live with Werner Output: Draft Overload Map
02 Wk 2
The recovery cycle — how depletion actually works

Sorensen’s Burnout Cycle as a working model. Identify where you currently sit in the cycle. Introduce the self-tracking dashboard and set your first weekly recovery target.

✓ Session 2 — live with Werner Dashboard: Week 1 baseline
Phase B — Recovery foundations
03 Wk 3
Sleep anchors and recovery rhythm

Build your sleep consistency foundation and downshift routine. Identify the two to three non-negotiable recovery practices that hold on a low-capacity week, not just a good one.

✓ Session 3 — live with Werner Dashboard: Sleep and recovery tracking
04 Wk 4
Micro-recovery and capacity management

Ultradian rhythm and planned recovery breaks. Build micro-recovery into your working day without requiring structural change. Review your first month of dashboard data: what the pattern is telling you.

✓ Session 4 — live with Werner Dashboard: Four-week pattern review
Phase C — Cognitive load and mental overload
05 Wk 5
Rumination and the “switched-on” pattern

CBT-informed tools for the thoughts that keep you running even when you are offline. Identify your specific rumination triggers. Build the first cognitive defusion practices from the ACT toolkit.

✓ Session 5 — live with Werner Dashboard: Workload and mental load tracking
06 Wk 6
Perfectionism, values, and the shutdown ritual

Address the perfectionism and values friction identified in your Overload Map. Build your end-of-day shutdown ritual and next-day top-three. Create the protected deep-work blocks that survive a hard week.

✓ Session 6 — live with Werner Dashboard: Boundary adherence tracking
Phase D — Boundaries, language, and the recovery protocol
07 Wk 7
Boundary scripts and capacity-signalling language

Capacity-signalling language, trade-off phrasing, and meeting-decline scripts. The specific words for when “no” feels impossible. Practice one real conversation this week before the final session.

✓ Session 7 — live with Werner Output: Draft Boundary Starter Pack
08 Wk 8
Personal Recovery Protocol — close-out

Consolidate your non-negotiables and early warning signs into your written Personal Recovery Protocol. Review eight weeks of dashboard data. Phase 2 pathway opens for those who are ready to redesign how they work.

✓ Session 8 — live with Werner Output: Personal Recovery Protocol Phase 2 pathway opens
