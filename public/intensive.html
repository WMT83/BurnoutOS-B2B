The problem with "just rest"

Rest recovers your energy. ACT recovers your relationship with work.

You've already tried taking a break. Maybe a weekend, maybe two weeks. You come back and within days you're right back to where you started — overwhelmed, exhausted, running on empty.

That's because burnout isn't caused by a deficit of rest. It's caused by a misalignment between your values and how you're actually spending your life. Rest treats the symptom. ACT treats the cause.

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy is the most extensively validated psychological framework for occupational burnout. It doesn't teach you to think more positively or manage your time better. It changes your relationship with difficult thoughts, emotions, and situations at a fundamental level.

The programme

8 weeks. 6 ACT pillars. One cohort.

Each session builds on the last. By week 8 you have a complete ACT toolkit and a personalised maintenance plan.

Week 1

Recognising burnout

Understand what burnout actually is — biologically, psychologically, and contextually. Burnout severity assessment debrief. Set your recovery intention.

Week 2

Values clarification

ACT pillar 1. Identify what matters most and how far you've drifted. Values become your compass for every decision ahead.

Week 3

Defusion from thoughts

ACT pillar 2. Learn to unhook from the relentless inner critic, perfectionism, and catastrophising that accelerate burnout.

Week 4

Acceptance of difficult states

ACT pillar 3. Stop fighting exhaustion, anxiety, and overwhelm. Radical acceptance as a recovery strategy — not resignation.

Week 5

Present-moment contact

ACT pillar 4. Mindfulness practices calibrated for burnout — not generic meditation, but targeted nervous system regulation.

Week 6

Self as context

ACT pillar 5. Rebuild a stable sense of self that isn't defined by your job title, productivity, or output.

Week 7

Committed action

ACT pillar 6. Design a sustainable work-life architecture. Boundaries, energy management, and re-engagement on your terms.

Week 8

Integration and continuation

Consolidate your ACT skills. Build your personalised maintenance plan. Cohort celebration and next-chapter commitments.
