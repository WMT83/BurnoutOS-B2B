Who this is for

You've tried resting. It didn't fix it.

The Intensive is designed for high-performing professionals who have already tried the obvious things — the holiday, the long weekend, the boundary-setting — and found themselves back in the same place within weeks. Rest recovers your energy. This programme recovers your relationship with work.

Clinicians and allied health professionals

Carrying others' load while running on empty. Compassion fatigue layered on top of structural burnout.

Leaders and senior managers

Responsible for everything, accountable to everyone. The job has expanded beyond what's sustainable.

Academics and educators

Chronic under-resourcing, high performance expectations, identity deeply tied to professional output.

Professionals in high-demand fields

Law, finance, consulting — environments where long hours and self-sacrifice are structural, not optional.

The journey

A structured recovery system — not just a course.

Each week combines a live group session with Werner, daily platform check-ins, and AI-supported practice between sessions. The learning doesn't stop when the Zoom call ends.

01
Before Week 1

Apply, enrol, and onboard

Complete your enrolment and receive your onboarding guide within 48 hours. Before Week 1 begins, you'll have a personal 15-minute call with Werner to map your specific burnout profile and set your recovery intention.

02
Weeks 1–8

Weekly live group sessions

One 90-minute session per week with Werner and your cohort. Small group — you'll know everyone by name within two weeks. Sessions are recorded; miss one without losing continuity. Specific time confirmed with applicants once Cohort 1 is full.

03
Every day

Daily check-ins and AI Coach

AM and PM prompts track your sleep, energy, and stress load. Your AI Coach adapts guidance based on your data and is available 24/7 between sessions for anything that comes up.

04

Sustain and grow

Build your Personal Operating Manual and 90-day sustainment plan. Platform access continues for 90 days post-programme so you can lock in lasting change before deciding on ongoing support.

The curriculum

8 weeks. Evidence-based. Outcome-focused.

Each session builds on the last. By Week 8 you have a complete toolkit and a personalised plan for sustaining what you've built.

Week 1 — 15 Jun

The Neuroscience of Burnout

Why your nervous system got here — and what it takes to recover. The biology behind chronic exhaustion, and how your specific pattern maps onto it.

Week 2 — 22 Jun

Trying Harder Is Making It Worse

The exhaustion trap keeping high performers stuck. Learn why your default coping strategies are deepening burnout, not resolving it.

Week 3 — 29 Jun

How to Stop the 3am Spiral

Breaking the rumination loop that steals your rest. Practical techniques for interrupting anxious thinking when it matters most.

Week 4 — 6 Jul

How to Reset Your Sleep

The science of nervous system recovery after burnout. Sleep window calibration, stimulus control, and wind-down protocols that work.

Week 5 — 13 Jul

Pivoting Towards Meaning in Work

Values-based recovery when everything feels pointless. Reconnect with what matters and use it to guide sustainable decisions.

Week 6 — 20 Jul

Effectively Managing Boundaries

Setting and maintaining boundaries without guilt. Scripts, frameworks, and communication strategies for real workplace situations.

Week 7 — 27 Jul

Sustainable Performance in the Long Run

Building lasting energy and performance after depletion. Capacity planning, early warning systems, and workload design.

Week 8 — 3 Aug

Your Personalised Relapse Prevention Plan

Your evidence-based long-term protection plan. Build your Personal Operating Manual and 90-day sustainment commitments.
