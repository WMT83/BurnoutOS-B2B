Some problems don't respond to group work.

They need a direct conversation with someone who has the clinical depth to see the pattern — and the coaching experience to help you shift it.

This is the programme for leaders, clinicians, founders, and senior operators who are clear their situation calls for one-to-one attention — either as a continuation after completing the Intensive, or as the right place to begin.

Ten sessions across five months. Fortnightly cadence. Structured enough that each session builds on the last. Long enough to see real change.

Who this is for

If this describes your situation, the fit call will likely confirm a match.

  • Graduates of the Burnout Recovery Intensive who want continued individual work
  • Senior professionals with specific leadership or performance challenges
  • People who value structured coaching over open-ended therapy
  • Professionals ready to commit to a five-month relationship, not a single-session fix
Who this is not for

If this describes your situation, coaching isn't the right frame.

  • People in acute clinical crisis
  • People seeking short-term support or a single-session fix
  • People seeking Medicare-rebatable therapy

If coaching isn't right for you, Werner will tell you directly on the fit call — and suggest what would be.

The engagement

Ten sessions. Five months. A structured arc.

Each phase has a deliberate purpose. The engagement builds toward a clear articulation of your signature pattern and the tools to act on it.

Before session 1

The fit call

A 20-minute conversation — free, structured, and honest. Werner assesses whether coaching is the right frame for what you're working on. Not everyone is offered a place. If coaching-appropriate, enrolment begins with a baseline pattern assessment using validated instruments from the clinical literature.

Sessions 1–4

Foundations

Pattern walkthrough and priority agreement. Three specific focus areas established. Early work on each.

Session 5

Mid-point re-check

Pattern re-assessment. Honest conversation about what's shifted, what hasn't, and whether the priorities still hold.

Sessions 6–8

Application

Priorities tested under live pressure. Real decisions, real demands, real integration work.

Session 9

Stocktake

Review of the engagement. Articulation of your signature pattern — the personal map you take forward.

Session 10

Integration and next steps

Honest close-out. New package, ad-hoc continuation, graduation, or alternative pathway — whichever is right.
