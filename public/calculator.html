Methodology

Calculations draw on Deloitte Australia (2023), Gallup State of the Global Workplace (2024), and Medibank Private cost-of-presenteeism research. Absenteeism modelled at 8 additional sick days/year per burnt-out employee. Presenteeism calculated as salary × burnout rate × productivity loss factor. Replacement costs estimated at 50–75% of annual salary depending on seniority. Healthcare uplift modelled at AUD 1,800/year per affected employee. All figures are indicative; the Psychosocial Risk Sprint provides organisation-specific data.
