How we calculate this

Three evidence-based cost categories, weighted by risk profile.

34%
Productivity loss
Burned-out employees operate at 34% below baseline productivity on average. Applied to salary as the cost of reduced output before absenteeism or resignation occurs.
4.2×
Absenteeism multiplier
Employees experiencing burnout take 4.2× more sick leave than peers. Calculated as additional days × daily salary cost, scaled by risk profile severity.
50–200%
Turnover replacement cost
Replacing a burned-out employee costs 50–200% of their annual salary (recruitment, onboarding, lost knowledge). The largest single driver of burnout cost in most organisations.
