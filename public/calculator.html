Methodology & sources
Productivity loss (34%)
Based on Gallup's State of the Global Workplace report and Maslach & Leiter's burnout cost research. Burned-out employees show a 34% reduction in productive output through presenteeism, reduced quality, and slower throughput.
Absenteeism (6.1 days)
Australian Bureau of Statistics data on unplanned absences. Employees with high burnout scores average 6.1 additional sick days per year compared to non-burned-out colleagues. Costed at daily salary equivalent.
Turnover cost (75%)
Burnout is the leading driver of voluntary turnover in professional services. Replacement cost (recruitment, onboarding, lost productivity during ramp-up) averages 75% of annual salary. We apply a 15% annual turnover rate among burned-out employees.
