The clinicians and senior professionals carrying the heaviest load don't usually need more information about burnout. They need a way to move when the system isn't going to change first.

What you'll learn

Six concrete capabilities you'll walk out with.

Each one is built around a template you'll complete in the workshop and refine over the following weeks.

01

Understand your Burnout Cycle

Use Sorensen's Burnout Cycle and the ten types of burnout to identify your own pattern. The triggers, the rules and "shoulds", the coping moves that keep the loop running.

02

Open up to difficult internal experiences

Practise openness and acceptance for the thoughts, emotions and sensations that come with chronic stress. The work is moving from struggle-and-avoidance toward willingness.

03

Handle difficult emotions with self-compassion

Build the emotional awareness and self-compassion skills that act as an antidote to the inner critic and to the self-blame that intensifies burnout.

04

Defuse from unhelpful burnout thoughts

Take perspective on the rules, shoulds, and self-stories that keep you stuck. Recognise thoughts as thoughts. Then choose what to do next.

05

Reconnect with your values

Identify what actually matters to you in your work and your life. Use values as the compass for direction. Sorensen's path from disengagement back to meaning and vitality.

06

Take committed values-based action

Translate values into daily habits and sustainable boundaries, and leave with a realistic 2 to 4 week implementation plan designed for low-capacity days.

FAQ

Things people ask before they register.

I can't make 26 June live. Does the recording still count for CPD?

Yes. You can attend live or watch the recordings on your own schedule. CPD points are issued on completion of the workshop content and the standard completion check. Not on live attendance specifically.

Is this workshop only for clinicians?

It's built primarily for clinicians and senior professionals in high-demand roles. That's where the moral distress, values conflict, and "no margin left" framing land. If your work involves consistent high responsibility and limited control over the workload, you're in the right room. CPD points are HPCSA-accredited for registered professionals, provided through the South African Medical Association (SAMA).

What's the format? Is it just a Zoom lecture?

No. It's a working session. Each block alternates teaching with a guided practice or template you complete in real time. You'll leave with completed materials, not a folder of slides.

What if I'm in active burnout right now?

This workshop is designed for working professionals navigating burnout. It's not a substitute for individual clinical care. If you're in crisis or your symptoms are severely impairing daily function, please connect with a treating clinician directly. You can still attend this workshop as part of your recovery plan, but it shouldn't be your only support.

What happens after I register?

You'll get an immediate confirmation email with your access details, a pre-work brief (about 20 minutes of light reading and one short reflection), and the calendar invite for the workshop and the follow-up Q&A.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. Full refund up to 7 days before the workshop. After that, you can transfer your seat to a future cohort or convert it into recordings-only access.

Can my employer pay for this?

Yes. We can issue a tax invoice in your or your employer's name. Reply to the confirmation email with the billing details and we'll handle it. For groups of 5 or more from the same organisation, write to hello@burnout-os.app.
