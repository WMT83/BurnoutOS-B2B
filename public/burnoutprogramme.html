The Programme

Eight weeks. One clear direction.

Weeks 1–2
Foundations & Assessment
Baseline burnout assessment, values clarification, introduction to the ACT model, and establishing your recovery baseline.
Weeks 3–4
Restoring Energy
Sleep restoration protocol, somatic regulation practices, pacing strategies, and reducing psychological overextension.
Weeks 5–6
Cognitive & Emotional Recovery
Defusion from burnout-related thinking patterns, emotional processing, boundary-setting through values, and rebuilding self-compassion.
Weeks 7–8
Sustainable Recovery
Building a long-term maintenance plan, identifying early warning patterns, and anchoring recovery practices into daily life.
