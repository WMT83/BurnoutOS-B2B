Why this matters commercially

The senior people you most need to retain are the ones least likely to ask for help.

By the time it shows up on an engagement survey, the resignation has already been written internally.

Standard wellbeing options aren't built for this population. EAP referrals carry stigma. Resilience workshops insult the audience. Coaching is slow to scope and clinically inconsistent.

This programme is contained, clinically led, and sits outside the EAP pathway. Two days in the room produces the shift. Eight weeks of integration is what stops it fading.

The integration phase · 8 weeks after the weekend

Eight weeks of structured application, mapped to the eight sessions.

Each session gets a week. Daily check-ins. AI prompts. Two cohort calls along the way. The work moves from the room into the working week.

Week 01

Noticing the neuroscience in real time

Daily check-ins track energy, sleep, rumination against baseline. First evidence of the patterns they came in with.

Week 02

Catching the over-effort patterns

Worksheets surface where over-effort is showing up. AI flags the strategies they're defaulting to. Prompts at the moments they're about to over-extend.

Week 03 Live call

Defusion practice and the first cohort call

Defusion techniques woven into daily check-ins. AI available for 3am support. 75-minute cohort call mid-week, naming the patterns the cohort is hitting.

Week 04

Sleep window calibration

Calibration from nightly tracking. Targeted protocols if baseline shows specific issues. Prompts for wind-down and morning anchors.

Week 05

Values mapping in real working time

Values work extended across the working week. AI flags aligned and misaligned moments as they happen.

Week 06 Live call

Boundary scripts and the second cohort call

Scripts tested in real conversations. 75-minute cohort call on the boundary work that's hardest: the conversations they've been avoiding.

Week 07

Capacity planning from real data

Built on six weeks of tracking. Designing the work architecture they'll carry forward.

Week 08

The relapse prevention plan

Built from 7 weeks of tracked data plus the weekend's content. Theirs to keep.
