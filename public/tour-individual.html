Why this workshop

CPD you can use in the room on Monday.

Burnout presents like a lot of other things. Low mood, poor sleep, irritability, cognitive slowing. Treated as depression it often does not shift, because the driver is still there every Monday morning.

The two days deliver the protocol. The eight sessions of the Performance Reset, worked through as a clinician. Assessment and formulation through to relapse prevention.

The eight weeks after are supervision. Three group sessions on your own burnout cases, where the protocol meets the caseload that keeps circling back.

32 CPD points. A sequence you have already used before you apply it in practice.

What you take back to practice

A protocol you can assess, formulate, and treat with.

Two days deliver the sequence. Three supervision sessions apply it to your own cases. The materials are yours to keep.

The two days · Sessions 1 to 8

Eight sessions across two days.

The full protocol is delivered here, worked through as a clinician. By Sunday evening you have the sequence and the materials.

Day 1 · Assessment and foundations
Sessions 1 to 4 · Saturday
Phase A

Assessment and formulation

Formulating burnout as a clinician. The drivers behind a presentation: demands, control, support, and values friction. Where it separates from depression.

Phase B

Load and recovery capacity

The physiology of depletion and recovery. Sleep anchors, micro-recovery, and the recovery practices that hold on a low-capacity week.

Day 2 · Intervention
Sessions 5 to 8 · Sunday
Phase C

Cognitive load and rumination

CBT-informed tools for rumination, perfectionism, and the switched-on pattern that runs even when the working day is over.

Phase D

Values, boundaries, and relapse prevention

Values-based intervention, capacity-signalling language, boundary work, and building a relapse prevention plan a client can hold.

After the two days · three group supervision sessions

Three supervision sessions on your own caseload, across eight weeks.

This is where the protocol meets your practice. Three facilitated group sessions across the eight weeks after the workshop, working your own burnout cases through the sequence.

Session 1 Group

Formulation on live cases

Bring a current burnout presentation from your own caseload. Map it against the protocol as a group and agree the formulation.

Session 2 Group

Intervention and sticking points

Where the sequence meets resistance in the room. The cases that are not moving, and how to hold them.

Session 3 Group

Relapse prevention and consolidation

Closing the loop on the cases carried through the eight weeks. Relapse prevention and what transfers to the rest of the caseload.
