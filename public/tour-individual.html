The weekend · Days 1 and 2

Eight sessions across two days.

The full protocol lives here. By Sunday evening you have the toolkit. The 8 weeks that follow are where it embeds.

Day 1 · Foundations
Sessions 1 to 4 · Saturday
Session 01

The Neuroscience of Burnout

Why the brain ends up here, and what shifts it. The biology of exhaustion — for you, and for the clients you'll be applying this with.

Session 02

Trying Harder Is Making It Worse

The exhaustion trap that keeps high performers stuck. Why the strategies that got you here can't get you out.

Session 03

How to Stop the 3am Spiral

Breaking the rumination loop that steals rest. Defusion and present-moment work for the nights the mind won't let go.

Session 04

How to Reset Your Sleep

Nervous system recovery after burnout. Sleep window calibration, stimulus control, wind-down protocols that work when you're depleted.

Day 2 · Integration
Sessions 5 to 8 · Sunday
Session 05

Pivoting Towards Meaning in Work

Values-based recovery when work feels pointless. Reconnecting with what matters and using it as the compass for the boundary and energy work that follows.

Session 06

Effectively Managing Boundaries in Relationships

Setting and holding boundaries without guilt. The blueprint for people who hate letting others down — at work, with family, with the colleagues you've been carrying.

Session 07

Sustainable Performance in the Long Run

Building energy and performance after depletion. Capacity planning, recovery rhythms, and the practical architecture of work that doesn't keep breaking you.

Session 08

Your Personalised Relapse Prevention Plan

The evidence-informed long-term protection plan, built in this final session. Specific to your context. The thing you take home and use.

The integration phase · 8 weeks after the weekend

Eight weeks of structured application, mapped to the eight sessions.

Each session gets a week. Daily check-ins, AI prompts, weekly worksheets. Two cohort calls along the way. The work moves from the room into your week.

Week 01

Noticing the neuroscience in your week

Daily check-ins track energy, sleep, and rumination against baseline. AI prompts surface the neuroscience as it unfolds. The first evidence of what you came in with.

Week 02

Catching the over-effort patterns

Worksheets surface where over-effort is showing up. AI reflection on the strategies you're defaulting to. Targeted prompts at the moments you're about to over-extend.

Week 03 Live call

Defusion practice and the first cohort call

Defusion techniques woven into daily check-ins. AI available for in-the-moment 3am support. 75-minute cohort call mid-week, naming the patterns the cohort is hitting.

Week 04

Sleep window calibration

Calibration from your nightly tracking data. Targeted protocols if your baseline shows specific issues. Prompts for wind-down and morning anchors.

Week 05

Values mapping in real working time

Values work extended across the week. AI flags aligned and misaligned moments as they happen, not in retrospect.

Week 06 Live call

Boundary scripts and the second cohort call

Scripts tested in real conversations, with AI debriefing what worked. 75-minute cohort call on the boundary work that's hardest in real life: the conversations you've been avoiding.

Week 07

Capacity planning from your data

Capacity planning built on six weeks of tracking. Designing the work architecture you'll carry forward.

Week 08

Your relapse prevention plan

Built from your 7 weeks of tracked data plus the weekend's content. The platform structures the construction. Yours to keep.

A note from the clinical lead
I built BurnoutOS because I wanted the programme I'd have benefited from ten years ago.

I'm a clinical psychologist. I treat burnout. I've also been burnt out — twice, both times while seeing burnt-out clients. The MBA I added afterwards was partly an intellectual response to that pattern and partly an attempt to outflank it. Neither was a clinical solution.

The clinical solution is the protocol you'll learn over these 10 weeks. The honest version of "learning it" is doing it. That's why the integration phase exists, and why this reads as much like a recovery arc as a CPD curriculum. For clinicians in this space, they're the same work.

If the dates and the shape suit you, I'd be glad to have you in the cohort.
